GEMB variables

Below is a list of variables used by GEMB.

VariableUnitsDescription
agedMass-weighted mean age, in decimal days, of all mass in a cell (ice/firn matrix plus pore water), measured from column initialization. Snowfall, rain and vapour deposition enter at age 0; meltwater carries the age of the firn it melted from. Accumulates across gemb_spinup cycles, so for a spun-up run the epoch is the start of spinup.
albedo_broadbandfractionTime series of broadband surface albedo (model output).
albedo_density_thresholdkg m<sup>-3</sup>Density threshold below which albedo_method is applied (Default Inf).
albedo_fixedfractionFixed albedo used if albedo_method="None" or density > threshold.
albedo_icefractionAlbedo for bare ice (0.2-0.6).
albedo_methodstringScheme: "GardnerSharp", "GreuellKonzelmann", etc.
albedo_snowfractionAlbedo for fresh snow (0.5-0.95).
aquifer_depthmDepth to the water table &mdash; the top of the shallowest cell holding more water than irreducible saturation &mdash; in the column saved as output at that instant. NaN when the column holds no standing water, so "dry" stays distinguishable from "water table at the surface". NaN throughout a run at the default runoff_method "instantaneous", under which no cell can exceed irreducible saturation.
aquifer_thicknessmTotal thickness of cells holding standing (super-irreducible) water, i.e. the saturated thickness a borehole would find wet, in the column saved as output at that instant. A cell counts as saturated once it exceeds irreducible by 1e-3 of its pore space, which excludes the negligible over-saturation left by compaction within a timestep; 0 throughout a run at the default runoff_method "instantaneous".
black_carbon_iceppmwblack carbon concentration in ice (parts per million by weight).
black_carbon_snowppmwblack carbon concentration in snow (parts per million by weight).
cloud_fractionfractionCloud fraction (0-1) for GreuellKonzelmann method.
cloud_optical_thicknessdimensionless
column_dzmaxmMaximum allowable grid spacing. See Figure 1 of Gardner et al., 2023
column_dzminmMinimum allowable grid spacing. See Figure 1 of Gardner et al., 2023
column_dztopmInitial surface grid spacing. See Figure 1 of Gardner et al., 2023
column_zmaxmMaximum total column depth. See Figure 1 of Gardner et al., 2023
column_zminmMinimum total column depth. See Figure 1 of Gardner et al., 2023
column_ztopmDepth of constant grid spacing at the surface. See Figure 1 of Gardner et al., 2023.
column_zymGrid stretching factor for lower layers. See Figure 1 of Gardner et al., 2023
densification_coeffs_M01stringCoefficients for Ligtenberg model (e.g., "GreRACMOGS_SW0").
densification_from_compactionmCompaction due to densification.
densification_from_meltmCompaction due to melt.
densification_methodstringModel: "HerronLangway", "Arthern", "ArthernB", "Barnola1991", "Crocus", "CrocusPure", "GSFC2020", "Simonsen2013", "Ligtenberg".
densitykg m<sup>-3</sup>Column density.
density_icekg m<sup>-3</sup>Density of pure glacier ice (default 917, matching the Community Firn Model and IMAU-FDM).
dzmLayer thickness profile.
elevationm
emissivityfractionBase longwave emissivity in the range of 0 to 1.
emissivity_grain_radius_largefractionEmissivity for large grain sizes, in the range of 0 to 1.
emissivity_grain_radius_thresholdmmGrain radius threshold for emissivity switching.
emissivity_methodstringMethod: "uniform", "grainradiusthreshold", "grainradiusw_threshold".
evaporation_condensationkg m<sup>-2</sup>Net vapour mass exchange, accumulated over the output interval: positive is mass gained by the column (condensation/deposition), negative is mass lost (evaporation/sublimation).
firn_air_contentmTotal height of all air in the firn column.
firn_air_content_10mmHeight of air in the top 10 m of the column, the depth-limited form the firn-core literature reports. The cell straddling 10 m contributes in proportion.
firn_air_content_20mmHeight of air in the top 20 m of the column.
grain_dendricityfractiongrain dendricity in the range of 0 to 1.
grain_growth_methodstringDry non-dendritic grain growth: "Arthern" (Arthern et al., 2010 &mdash; the default, as shipped by the Community Firn Model; grows at every density), "Marbouty" (Marbouty, 1980 &mdash; temperature-gradient driven, but stops dead above 400 kg m<sup>-3</sup>), or "hybrid" (Marbouty below that ceiling, Arthern at and above it). Wet cells take the Brun branch on every setting.
grain_radiusmmEffective grain radius.
grain_sphericityfractiongrain sphericity in the range of 0 to 1.
heat_flux_latentW m<sup>-2</sup>Latent heat flux (positive toward surface).
heat_flux_sensibleW m<sup>-2</sup>Sensible heat flux (positive toward surface).
horizontal_strain_rateyr<sup>-1</sup>Horizontal strain rate, the trace of the horizontal strain-rate tensor (&epsilon;&#775;<sub>xx</sub> + &epsilon;&#775;<sub>yy</sub>). Positive is divergence, which thins every layer at constant density; negative is convergence. Default 0, which disables the term.
ice_slab_depthmDepth to the top of the shallowest flow-blocking ice slab, in the column saved as output at that instant. NaN when no slab qualifies, so "no slab" stays distinguishable from "slab at the surface".
ice_slab_thicknessmTotal thickness of cells at or above impermeable_density, whether or not they block flow, in the column saved as output at that instant.
impermeable_densitykg m<sup>-3</sup>Density at or above which a cell's pore space is treated as disconnected, so percolating water cannot pass a sufficiently thick run of such cells (default 830). Across the RetMIP models this criterion spans 810 to 917.
impermeable_thicknessmMinimum contiguous thickness of cells at or above impermeable_density for the run to block percolation and route water to runoff (default 0.1). Below this, water passes through: thin lenses are laterally discontinuous at the scale the model represents.
latent_heatJ kg<sup>-1</sup>Latent heat of vaporization or sublimation used.
latitudedegreesGeographic coordinate in decimal degrees.
longitudedegreesGeographic coordinate in decimal degrees.
longwave_downwardW m<sup>-2</sup>Downward longwave radiation.
longwave_netW m<sup>-2</sup>Net longwave radiation.
mass_addedkg m<sup>-2</sup>Mass added (positive) or removed (negative) by manage_layers.
meltkg m<sup>-2</sup>Melt mass
melt_geometrystringWhat melting does to a cell's geometry: "thickness" (the default &mdash; density held fixed and dz shrinks, as in Crocus) or "density" (dz held fixed and density falls, as in SNOWPACK). Refreezing is at constant thickness either way, so only "density" makes a melt&ndash;refreeze cycle return the cell's geometry along with its mass. Fourteau et al. (2026) Sect. 2.3 argues for "density": the phase change occurs within the microstructure, and the high density of wet snow is better explained by its low viscosity under overburden than by melt-driven thinning.
new_snow_methodstringFresh snow density model (e.g., :Constant350, :Fausto, :Pahaut). :Pahaut is Pahaut (1975) as Crocus implements it, the one option fitted to alpine seasonal snow rather than to a polar ice sheet &mdash; prefer it for temperate and mid-latitude glaciers.
output_frequencystringOutput resolution: "daily", "monthly", "all", or "last".
output_paddinginteger valueExtra vertical levels for grid resizing (default: 1000).
percolation_depthmDeepest the meltwater wetting front reached during the output interval (an interval maximum, not a mean). Comparable to upward-looking-radar estimates. Zero when no water moved.
precipitationkg m<sup>-2</sup> or mmweMass of precipitation that falls within each time step.
pore_saturation_maxfractionCap on how full a cell may get when water ponds above a barrier, as a fraction of its pore space (default 1.0, i.e. full saturation permitted). Capacity is (density_ice &minus; density)&middot;pore_saturation_max&middot;dz, the same form irreducible retention uses, so water/capacity is the saturation water_irreducible_saturation is defined against. Only read when runoff_method is not "instantaneous".
precipitation_meankg m<sup>-2</sup>Climatologial mean precipitation per time step
pressure_airPaAir pressure
ProfiletableTable output by model_initialize_profile and input into gemb containing nine variables that describe the initial state of the snow/firn/ice column.
rainkg m<sup>-2</sup>Rain mass.
rain_temperature_thresholdKTemperature threshold above which precipitation falls as rain.
refreezekg m<sup>-2</sup>Refrozen mass
relative_humiditypercentRelative humidity in the range of 0 to 100 percent.
run_prefixstringUnique run identifier (default: "default").
runoffkg m<sup>-2</sup>Runoff mass
runoff_methodstringHow water that percolation cannot pass leaves the column: "instantaneous" (the default &mdash; blocked water runs off within the time step and no cell can exceed irreducible saturation), "ZuoOerlemans" (a slope-dependent characteristic timescale, &tau; = c<sub>1</sub> + c<sub>2</sub>&middot;exp(&minus;c<sub>3</sub>&middot;S)), or "Darcy" (a saturated-conductivity flux down the slope). The latter two let water pond above a barrier and drain over a finite timescale, which is what makes standing water and firn aquifers representable; both are scaled by surface_slope.
shortwave_absorption_methodTO BE CHANGED
shortwave_downwardW m<sup>-2</sup>Downward shortwave radiation.
shortwave_downward_diffuseW m<sup>-2</sup>Downward diffusive shortwave flux.
shortwave_netW m<sup>-2</sup>Net shortwave radiation.
solar_zenith_angledegreesSolar zenith angle.
strain_thinningmColumn thickness change from horizontal ice-dynamic strain (positive is thinning). Zero unless horizontal_strain_rate is set.
surface_roughness_effective_ratioRatio of physical surface roughness to effective roughness.
surface_slopem m<sup>-1</sup>Surface slope, the lateral hydraulic gradient driving runoff under runoff_method "ZuoOerlemans" or "Darcy" (default 0, unread under "instantaneous"). A gradient, not degrees: the RetMIP sites span 0 (Summit) to 0.6 degrees (the firn-aquifer site), i.e. 0 to about 0.010 m m<sup>-1</sup>. "Darcy" requires a positive value, since its flux is proportional to the slope.
temperatureKColumn temperature.
temperature_airKAir temperature.
temperature_air_meanKClimatologial average temperature.
temperature_observation_heightmThermometer height.
thermal_conductivity_methodstringModel: "Calonne2019" (Calonne et al., 2019 eq. 5; the default &mdash; a sigmoid blend at 450 kg m<sup>-3</sup> between snow and firn branches, scaled by the temperature-dependent conductivity of ice, and continuous into ice), "Calonne2019Air" (the same equation carrying the air-conductivity ratio on its snow branch, as IMAU-FDM does), "Sturm" (Sturm et al., 1997), "Calonne" (Calonne et al., 2011 &mdash; density only), or "Marchenko2019" (Marchenko et al., 2019 eq. 30, floored at the Calonne 2011 value below their fitted density range). RetMIP recommends the 2019 formulations, and the Community Firn Model and IMAU-FDM both ship one.
thickness_cumulativemProfile thickness accounting for mass added and subtracted from the system.
timedatetimeDateTime-indexed times (a datenum-style numeric format is also accepted).
valid_profile_lengthLength of the vertical profile at the instant in time it was saved as output.
vapor_pressurePaVapor pressure.
waterkg m<sup>-2</sup>Pore water
water_irreducible_methodstringIrreducible water retention: "ColeouLesaffre" (Col&eacute;ou &amp; Lesaffre, 1998 via Langen et al., 2017 eq. 4 &mdash; the default; saturation rises as porosity closes, and is zero at and above pore closeoff), or "constant" (a flat water_irreducible_saturation at every density). RetMIP recommends a density-dependent form.
water_irreducible_saturationfractionIrreducible water content saturation fraction (0-0.2, default 0.07). Read only when water_irreducible_method is "constant".
wind_observation_heightmHeight above the surface where wind is measured or modeled.
wind_speedm s<sup>-1</sup>Wind speed.
wind_speed_meanm s<sup>-1</sup>Climatological average wind speed.

Author Information

The Glacier Energy and Mass Balance (GEMB) was created by Alex Gardner, with contributions from Nicole-Jeanne Schlegel and Chad Greene. Complete code and documentation are available at https://github.com/alex-s-gardner/GEMB. Please cite any use of GEMB as:

Gardner, A. S., Schlegel, N.-J., and Larour, E.: Glacier Energy and Mass Balance (GEMB): a model of firn processes for cryosphere research, Geosci. Model Dev., 16, 2277–2302, https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-16-2277-2023, 2023.
